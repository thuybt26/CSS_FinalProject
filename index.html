Welcome to Springtime

Springtime in Ann Arbor is a beautiful time of year. Even though it can be chilly at times, it is wonderful to explore the outdoors and enjoy the fresh colors of spring.

This website shares some of the places and sights that make spring walks around Ann Arbor special. From neighborhood streets to peaceful parks, every walk offers something interesting to see.
